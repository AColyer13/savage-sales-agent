# 05 Deal Inspection and Red Flags

Run it on a deal: answer each question Yes/No with evidence, and write a specific next step for every No.
Total 30 questions (10 per Why). Be honest; "I think so" is a No until you can point to evidence.

## WHY ANYTHING
1. Do we fully understand the prospect's core problem?
2. Is the problem a priority for our target market?
3. Have we seen similar challenges with other clients, and how did we address them?
4. Is their expectation for a solution realistic for what we offer?
5. Any gaps in our understanding of their pain points?
6. How does this align with our product roadmap and capabilities?
7. Do we have the resources to address this effectively?
8. Is there a clear ROI for them?
9. Any external factors influencing their problem we haven't considered?
10. Have we validated the problem through multiple stakeholders?

## WHY US
11. What unique value do we bring that competitors don't?
12. Have we clearly communicated our unique selling proposition?
13. Any concerns/objections we haven't fully addressed?
14. How does the prospect perceive our brand/product vs. the competition?
15. Do we clearly understand their selection criteria?
16. Any features/services they need that we lack?
17. How committed are we to adapting our solution to their needs?
18. Have we used all available testimonials, case studies, data?
19. Is our pricing aligned with the perceived value?
20. Have we identified all decision-makers and influencers and understood their perspectives?

## WHY NOW
21. Do we understand the external/internal factors driving their timeline?
22. Is there genuine urgency, or is the timeline flexible?
23. How does this deal align with our sales targets and timelines?
24. Any foreseeable challenges meeting their expected timeline?
25. Have we identified events/triggers that accelerated the need?
26. Is their timeline realistic for the current stage of the process?
27. How does their buying cycle compare with our sales cycle?
28. Any dependencies/prerequisites that could delay implementation or adoption?
29. Have we allocated the resources to meet their deadline?
30. Is there a risk of stalling, and have we planned contingencies?

## Grading
The course sheet lists two scales that conflict (top: SAVAGE 26+, CHAMPION 21-25, PROFESSIONAL 15-20, AMATEUR <15;
bottom, copied from the discovery sheet: 30+/23-29/18-22/<18). With 30 questions, use the first scale
(26+ / 21-25 / 15-20 / <15 Yeses) and tell the user you did. Also report each Why's subscore (/10): the weakest Why is the focus.

## Turning red flags green: 6 questions that drive action
1. **What's been communicated?** Have all internal stakeholders (product, sales, CS) seen the red flags?
2. **What can be done?** A clear actionable plan per red flag (more info, adjust offering, bring in senior leadership).
3. **Do we need to pivot?** Adjust value proposition/offering to the prospect's needs (tailored approach, added services)?
4. **How can we use past success?** Testimonials, case studies, data that speak to the specific concern.
5. **What's the competition?** What are their alternatives if the flag can't be fully addressed, and how do we position against them?
6. **What's been done?** Steps already taken. Action is the equalizer: the faster you act on a red flag, the better your odds.

## Output format for an inspection
Deal name / value / close date -> table of the 30 (Yes/No, evidence, next step) -> subscores per Why -> grade -> top 3 red flags -> the 6-question action plan -> the single most important next step this week with an owner and date.
