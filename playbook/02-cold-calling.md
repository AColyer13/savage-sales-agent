# 02 Cold Calling: "Million Dollar Cold Call Script" template

Memorize it ("even actors have lines"). Tonality is key. Goal of the call: book the next meeting (within 48 hours if possible).
Rank inbound 1-5 and work it by priority.

## Getting to the conversation
```
DM: Hi this is XXX, how may I help you?
You: "Hi, is the [DM TITLE or FIRST NAME] around?"      (if you don't know the name: "Great, what was your name?")
You: "Do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling?"
You: "The reason I'm reaching out is I was wondering if [RELEVANT PAIN POINT] was something that's top of mind for you right now?"
     -> WAIT FOR THEM TO ACKNOWLEDGE
You: "Well the [SIMILAR TITLES/COMPANIES] we work with are [POSITIVE OUTCOME], but aren't doing it through [COMMON OBJECTION/EXPENSIVE WAY].
      They're [HIGH-LEVEL HOW + OUTCOME]. So I was curious to learn [HOW THEY ADDRESS SIMILAR PAIN TODAY]?"
```

## Pain-probing questions (per business metric)
1. First-person storytelling question to expose process gaps ("If I came in as a new customer, what happens to make sure I...?")
2. Same question from a different perspective if the answer wasn't clear.
3. Question from the DM's perspective: how does this gap affect their ability to do their job? ("how do you know if...")
4. Layering question to size the business impact (rates, counts, what an average customer is worth).
5. Concerned tonality: how long has it been a problem, what are they doing about it?
6. Ask if they'd be open to hearing what others in their position are doing about it.
**Usually 1 pain point is enough to book a meeting. Don't overthink it.**

## Common objections and responses (fill [brackets] from profile.md; never invent stats)
- **Abrupt "not interested" before the pitch**: "That's fair. I have only one question to see if it would be relevant. [DIRECT PAIN QUESTION they'd say yes to]? [results you deliver for others]. Wouldn't it at least be worth a quick conversation to see if it's worth exploring further?"
- **"What's this about?" (gatekeeper or unknown)**: DO NOT PITCH. "[Relevant pain point, one short phrase]. Are you the [DM role]?"
- **"No thanks / busy / no time"**: "Well let me ask you a quick question before I let you go? [Question that highlights the pain]? And [what are you doing to solve it]? Perhaps it's worth spending a couple minutes to learn how other [similar customers] are solving this. How does that sound?"
- **"We have a tool for that already"**: "Do you have more than one way to [massive pain/goal they already pursue several ways]? [Show you know they use multiple approaches.] The same goes for [your pain point]. [Results for other clients]. Wouldn't it be worth exploring other options in case we can [help them like their competition]?" Then ask what tool, how long, what they love, what challenges they have (dig for pain).
- **"[Pain point] isn't a priority"**: "Let me ask you a quick question. Did you know [third-party data point that makes it a priority]? Perhaps it's worth a couple minutes to learn [how competition is getting ahead]. What do you say?"
- **"We use [competitor]"**: "That's great, they're a great solution. Tell me what you love about [competitor]? And if you could change anything, what would it be? Interesting, why that?" (Dig for pain.)
- **"I'm not the best person to talk to"**: "I'd like to challenge you on that a little, and I'll tell you why. Often the processes we help with are already being done, but manually, by people in your role, so it's your time that's getting lost. How about you and I connect for a few minutes, and if it's valuable we'll figure out next steps together?"
- **"Budget"**: "Let me ask a quick question: [1-2 questions that magnify the problem]. If budget weren't an issue, would this be worth discussing? [Payback period / often no new budget needed]. I don't know your situation enough yet, but does that make you more comfortable having a preliminary conversation?"
- **"About to go into a meeting / call me back"**: "One quick question to see if it's worth calling back: [direct pain question]. Appreciate you sharing. Since you [how they responded], how about 5 minutes this afternoon where I share how we help [people like them] [payoff]? When's the best time for a 5-minute call?"
- **"Send me an email"**: "I can. Just so I'm not wasting your time, what would make that email a 10 out of 10 for you?" Generic/no answer: "Typically our customers are most interested in [problems you solve]. Is that something you'd like to learn about? If you have 2 minutes, let's discuss now, and I'll send more if you want." Specific answer: "Interesting, I'm curious why that?" (dig for pain) then same 2-minute offer.
- **"That decision comes from corporate"**: "Completely understand. We can cross the corporate bridge later. We've talked to a lot of [people in your position] in the same boat, but after seeing how we can [help their position specifically] it made sense to the business. I realize it may not be your decision, but would you be open to seeing if we'd even be a good fit? I wouldn't reach out to corporate unless you felt it would personally benefit you."

## Anyone but the decision maker (gatekeeper / influencer)
```
GK: "She just stepped out, can I take a message?"
You: "I can try another time, what time are they usually in? Maybe you can help me out. I'm trying to learn [pain you help solve related to them].
      What's your role at [company]? Do you handle [responsibilities that make them sound important]?"
GK: "Yes, I'm XXXX"
You: "Thank you for sharing. We partner with [companies like them] to help [benefit they'd care about]. I'm sure you know a ton about what's going on
      since you [their responsibilities]. Could you do me the biggest favor? Would you mind chatting for 2 minutes? I'd like a quick understanding of
      [how something they can speak to works] to see if it's even worth reaching out to [DM]. Do you have 2 minutes?"
```

## Executing the qualification conversation
Reminders: **do not move to business pain if you don't have process pain.** One big-enough pain is enough to sell the next step. Make it two-way.
- Open (if pain isn't clear): "To help us focus our time together, what is the biggest pain point you're looking to solve right now?"
- **Process pain**: How do you [process] today? What's the challenge with it? Can you give me a specific example? Why do you think that's happening?
- **Business pain (magnify)**: How long has this been a problem? What have you done to try to solve it? Any insight into what it's costing you? (Ask for counts, rates, value of an average customer.)
- **Ideal solution**: What would an ideal solution look like in your eyes? Would that address [business cost they named]?
- If the pain is **time**: "The biggest thing we hear from [role] is they don't have enough time for [list]. Which of those takes more time than you'd like each week?" then process, cause, how long it takes, ideal solution.

## Closing the next step
DO NOT try to sell the next step without pain. Be specific; vague is meaningless.
"Thank you for sharing all of this. [RECAP their findings]. A lot of what you described sounds similar to how we helped [CASE STUDY]. We did that by [match their findings to your solution + case study results; BE SPECIFIC]. Based on this being a priority, I think it's worth a deeper dive to see if we can help you like [other customers]. I'd love to schedule [NEXT STEP and what it entails]. How does [a time within 48 hours] sound?"

**Hard objection/hard no** (not just rescheduling): softly, "Interesting... can you help me understand what's driving your hesitation?" Then: "I'm a bit confused. A minute ago you mentioned [PROBLEM] was something you're interested in figuring out, and it sounds like there's a good chance we can help. Did I miss something?"

## Setting expectations (if yes)
Thank them. Confirm date/time and that you're sending the invite. Confirm attendees and ask who else should join. Confirm email + phone.
Share purpose, agenda, outcome. Say you'll send a recap email with next steps.
**Post-close**: "Is there anything that would prevent you from joining?" (If yes: "all I ask is that you communicate with me so I can reschedule properly.")
Get their cell: "To make it easy I'm going to give you my cell so if you need to reschedule you can text me. What's your cell? I'll text you real quick so you have it. Sent. Did you get it? Great."
