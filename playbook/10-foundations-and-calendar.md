# 10 Foundations and Calendar (the "First Call" exercises)

Five personal exercises from the course. Rules common to all: follow the instructions exactly, don't skip ahead, distraction-free, get your blood flowing first
(at least 15 minutes of exercise to raise your heart rate), time-box each question (never less than the stated minutes). The user's answers are PERSONAL:
coach through them, but only save answers to a file if the user asks, and never put them in profile.md or deals/.

## 1. Purpose (60-90 min)
5+ minutes per question, then write a 1-2 sentence **purpose statement** and put it where you see it every day.
1 What brings me joy? 2 What am I passionate about? 3 What are my strengths (what do others compliment)? 4 What are my values? 5 What impact do I want to make?
6 What were my childhood dreams? 7 What makes time disappear? 8 What do I want to be known for (legacy)? 9 What challenges have I overcome?
10 What inspires me in others? 11 What would I do even without pay? 12 What would I regret not doing? 13 What makes me different? 14 What aligns with my intuition?
15 Summarize all of it in 1-2 sentences MAX = purpose statement.

## 2. Current State Clarity (30 min)
Score 1-10 in six areas, write a couple of sentences on WHY for each score, then (5+ min each) describe in detail what a **10/10** looks like. Average the scores.
Areas and the self-assessment prompts: **Body** (physical feel, energy, sleep, diet, exercise) / **Relationships** (communication, support, trust, being yourself, shared experiences) /
**Time** (prioritizing, goals and deadlines, handling distractions, time blocking, reflect and adjust) / **Career** (satisfaction, room to grow, company values fit, work-life integration, fulfillment) /
**Finances** (within means, budget, debt, savings/investing, emergencies) / **Experiences** (cherished memories, new experiences, regrets, how they shaped me, creating meaningful memories).

## 3. Gratitude (60-90 min)
10 minutes of continuous writing per area, no skipping around; smaller things are better ("I'm grateful my wife asks about my day").
Areas: relationships, finances, career, health, spirituality, home living situation, life experiences.

## 4. Cost of Inaction (30 min; will be uncomfortable by design)
Do it after Purpose and Current State. 5+ minutes per question, then write what has to happen to avoid that future, and keep it nearby. Why: future pain pulls you through adversity in a way future pleasure can't.
1 Which dreams will I regret not pursuing? 2 Where will stagnation lead me (who am I letting down)? 3 What missed opportunities will haunt me? 4 How will procrastination impact my future?
5 Which choices will I regret? 6 How will my vision be unfulfilled? 7 What opportunities will slip away? 8 What will my unconquered fears cost me? 9 Who will I admire for taking action?
10 How will regret shape my self-worth? 11 Who am I letting down by not taking action (what will they think; how will that affect feeling I lived up to my potential)? 12 Summarize in 1-2 sentences MAX = cost of inaction summary.
Link to selling: this is the personal version of the buyer's "what happens if this problem isn't solved?" question. Use it to feel why the cost of inaction question matters.

## 5. Master Your Calendar
Fact: the average salesperson spends only about 35% (about 2 hours a day) of their time selling.
**Steps**: review the last 1-2 weeks of your calendar and color-code it; note revenue vs non-revenue time; implement time blocks; tell your manager (get support); commit daily; share wins.
**Four categories and targets**
| Category | Target | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Direct revenue generating | 60% (30+ hrs/wk) | cold calling, cold email, social prospecting, scheduled customer calls, existing-opportunity follow-up |
| Indirect revenue generating | 25% (about 12 hrs/wk) | lead generation, research, prospecting prep, customer call prep, planning your day, content creation |
| Non-revenue | 5% (under 3 hrs/wk) | internal meetings that aren't enablement, checking email/Slack/social, lunch/breaks, admin |
| Growth | 10% (about 5 hrs/wk) | film review/practice, podcasts/reading, training, coaching |
**Best practices**: block the calendar with colors and make it hard for others to book you; prospect first thing in the morning while energy is high; prospecting is priority #1;
check email/Slack/phone only at designated times; take breaks at set times; say no to non-revenue meetings; plan the NEXT day the day before (not the morning of);
tell your manager and be transparent; do a weekly review of how well you stuck to it.
**Example "Day in the Life"** (from the course image): 8-10 prospecting / 10:00 break, email, Slack / 10:30-12 prospecting / 12:00 lunch / 12:30-3:00 scheduled customer calls and follow-ups /
3-4 lead generation and research / 4:00 next-day planning / 4:30 break, email, Slack / 5-6 self development.
**Manager message template**: "Hey boss. I'm taking action to help me be more productive so I can bring in more revenue for the business. A big part of what's going to help me be more productive is reworking my calendar and putting in dedicated time blocks on the most important activities. In our next 1-1 can I walk you through the framework I'm implementing to get your feedback?"
