# 03 Discovery

## The 30 questions (3 Whys)
**WHY ANYTHING**: What prompted you to start looking for a solution? / Can you describe the challenges you're currently facing? / What goals are you trying to achieve this quarter/year? / How is this issue affecting your team/organization? / What happens if this problem isn't solved? / Have you tried any solutions in the past? / What's your ideal outcome? / How are you measuring success regarding this problem? / What do you think is missing in your current approach? / How does this challenge align with your overall business objectives?

**WHY US**: What's most important to you in a solution? / Have you used similar solutions in the past? / What features are you looking for? / How do you see our solution fitting into your current workflow? / What concerns do you have about our product/service? / In what ways do you think our solution can help you achieve your goals? / How do you evaluate the success of a new tool or solution? / What's your process for deciding on a new solution? / Can you share any experiences with competitors' solutions? / What do you find most appealing about our solution?

**WHY NOW**: What has changed that makes this a priority now? / Is there a specific event or deadline driving this decision? / How does this initiative align with your current priorities? / What are the consequences of delaying this decision? / How quickly are you hoping to implement a solution? / What steps need to be taken before you can move forward? / Who else is involved in making this decision? / What's your timeline for making a decision? / Are there any obstacles that could delay this project? / How can we help make this transition as smooth as possible?

Pick 3-5 per Why, not all 30. Use FALL to layer. Pace it so it isn't an interrogation.

## Discovery flow (what the scorecard expects)
Research and relevant rapport -> introduce yourself with credibility and what's in it for them -> frame the meeting (purpose, plan, outcome; get agreement) -> recap pain/goals already uncovered -> ask how the business is doing -> how important are the goals / how long on their mind -> consequences of not solving -> how they're solving it now -> **ask them to quantify** -> tailored story of business value -> simplicity of onboarding and time to value -> ask if the story resonates -> budget and who signs off. Duration: more than 5 and under 20 minutes (tailor to meeting length).

## Discovery Assessment scorecard (36 points; score honestly, one specific fix per "no")
**Mindset and intangibles (10)**: paused before responding; no assumptions; detached from closing; balanced give/take (not an interrogation); used their language and goals; kept control without unproductive tangents; slowed down; smooth, calm, relaxed tone; casual/conversational; concise.
**Discovery execution (15)**: relevant researched rapport; introduced self with credibility and what's-in-it-for-them; set the stage (purpose, plan, outcome, agreed); recapped prior pain/goals; asked how the business is doing; asked how important goals are / how long on mind; asked consequences of not solving; asked how they're solving it; asked them to quantify; used a specific tailored story; spoke to onboarding simplicity and time to value; asked if the story resonates; discussed budget and sign-off; lasted >5 min; lasted <20 min.
**Handling adversity (5)**: paused after the objection; sounded confident; sought to understand before responding; put the ball back in their court; didn't grasp at straws to convince.
**Next steps (6)**: confirmed value before the demo (if same call); asked for another meeting if someone else is needed; asked for cell; said recap email + calendar invite are coming; sent recap email (pain uncovered, how we help, purpose of next meeting); sent tailored calendar invite with purpose.
**Grades (course scale)**: SAVAGE 30+, CHAMPION 23-29, PROFESSIONAL 18-22, AMATEUR <18 (of 36).

## Persona-aware discovery
Different personas care about different things. Ask what a day in their life looks like, what they struggle with, and their top 2-3 goals. Plan for detractors (who might fear this hurts their job).
