# 08 SUPPLEMENT: Outreach and follow-up (NOT from the course)
Source: general B2B best practice (see SOURCES.md). Evidence is mostly vendor benchmarks and they disagree, so treat numbers as
directional and A/B test your own. Style stays Savage: pain before pitch, curious not salesy, always a next step.

## Cold email (course gap: course only teaches phone)
- **Length**: body about 50-125 words; follow-ups 30-50 words. Short subject lines (about 2-7 words, no clickbait).
- **Structure**: (1) a reason this person specifically (research or trigger), (2) one pain stated as a question or observation in their language,
  (3) one line of proof (real, from profile.md), (4) one low-friction ask. No attachments, no feature dump.
- **Reuse the phone opener**: "I was wondering if [PAIN] was something that's top of mind. The [similar roles] we work with are [OUTCOME] without [COMMON EXPENSIVE WAY]."
- **Sequence**: about 4-7 touches over about 2 weeks (example: day 0, 3, 6, 10, 14). Each follow-up adds something new (a case study, a question, a data point), never "just bumping this".
  Mix channels with calls. Last touch is a polite breakup.
- **Measure positive reply rate, not opens** (open tracking is unreliable). Benchmarks to sanity check: median cold reply rate is roughly 1-2%. Test one variable at a time, ideally 250+ contacts per variant.
- **Persona-specific**: write a separate message per persona (the course's persona exercise).

## LinkedIn
Connection note: no pitch, one line of genuine relevance. After they accept: a question about their pain, not a pitch. Voice notes and comments on their posts beat templates.

## Recap email after every call (the course says send one but gives no template)
Send within a few hours. Format:
```
Subject: Recap + next step: [their goal]
Hi [name], thanks for the time today. What I heard:
- Your goal: [their words]
- What's in the way: [pain, with their numbers if they gave them]
- What it's costing: [impact they quantified]
Where we landed: [agreed next step], [date/time], with [attendees].
Purpose / agenda / outcome for that meeting: [3 bullets]
Anything I missed or got wrong? [one line]
```
Calendar invite carries the same purpose/agenda/outcome. Never invent numbers they didn't say.

## Voicemail (30 seconds)
Name, one pain-led reason, one proof line, when you'll try again, your number twice. Don't ask for a callback as the only outcome; pair it with an email.

## Inbound lead ranking 1-5 (RECONSTRUCTED, not the course)
The course notes mention an "inbound ranking 1-5" chart, but the chart is not in the folder and no public version exists. This is a general-practice stand-in
(fit = ICP match, intent = what they did and how recently). Replace it if you find the original.
| Rank | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Strong ICP fit + high intent (demo/pricing request, replied) | Call now; book a meeting in the first conversation |
| 4 | Good fit + moderate intent (webinar, repeat visits) | Call/email same day; reference what they did |
| 3 | Fit, intent unclear | Short outreach sequence; qualify pain first |
| 2 | Weak fit or low intent (single content download) | Nurture; low-touch only |
| 1 | Not ICP / spam / student / competitor | Disqualify |
Score fit and intent separately, then combine. Sources are third-party lead-scoring guides (see SOURCES.md).
