# 06 Business Case

## Before you build one (3 steps)
1. **CONFIRM you have a champion**: gets you access to power; has skin in the game; has proven they're in.
2. **VALIDATE the scope and next steps**: right use case (don't whale hunt); you can actually solve their problem; what happens after is clearly defined.
3. **COLLABORATE on building it**: no surprises; work with your champion; share drafts and get feedback.

**Champion conversation (do it in a live deal):**
- "Do you have any personal gains if this project is successful?"
- "Are you sold on us as the vendor of choice at this point?" If not: "What are your hesitations?"
- If yes to both: "Are you willing to be my champion to help me get this across the finish line?"

## Delivering it (3 steps)
1. **ASSUME nothing**: does it make sense to THEM, in THEIR language, conservative, realistic, and do they believe it?
2. **ALIGN everyone beforehand**: make your rounds before the executive readout (leverage your champion); get feedback individually (no large group calls); align to each person's individual goals; move neutral people to yes and negative people to neutral.
3. **HERO**: make them the hero. The slide's sub-point is an image of **The Hero's Journey** (the 12-stage story arc, labeled: Ordinary World, Call to Adventure, Refusal of the Call, Meet the Mentor, Cross the First Threshold, Tests/Allies/Enemies, Approach to the Innermost Cave, Ordeal, Reward, The Road Back, Resurrection, Return with the Elixir; Acts 1-3).
   So the teaching is: tell the business case as the champion's hero story, with the champion as hero and **you as the mentor**. The course recording isn't public, so the stage-by-stage mapping below is INFERRED, not Justin's words; use the user's own notes if they have them.
   - Ordinary World = current state and what it's costing. Call to Adventure = the trigger / Why Now. Refusal of the Call = fear of change, status-quo objections.
   - Meet the Mentor = you guiding with proof and a clear process (the MAP). Cross the First Threshold = agree to evaluate/pilot.
   - Tests, Allies, Enemies = evaluation, fans vs skeptics (stakeholder map). Innermost Cave / Ordeal = security, legal, procurement, the executive readout.
   - Reward = the win/approval. The Road Back / Resurrection = implementation and first proof it works. Return with the Elixir = results the champion shares, credit and recognition for them.
   Practical use: open the readout with the ordinary world in THEIR words, make the champion visibly the person who found and drove the solution, and give them the credit line.
See also playbook/09 for the action-based champion test and economic-buyer pre-read.

## Business case skeleton
Problem and cost of inaction (their numbers) -> goals/success criteria in their language -> proposed solution scoped to the use case -> conservative ROI with assumptions stated and sourced -> risks and how they're addressed -> stakeholder alignment status -> mutual action plan to decision -> the ask.
Use only numbers the buyer gave or that are sourced in profile.md; mark all assumptions.
