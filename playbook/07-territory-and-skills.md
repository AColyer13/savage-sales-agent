# 07 Territory, ICP, Personas, Skills

## Run it like a CEO of your business
- **Plan = P / C / V**: Process, Conversion, Velocity. Know the numbers to hit quota. Revisit the conversion funnel quarterly.
- **Chess vs Checkers**: apply skill to the deal. Deal size? Number of products? Stakeholders (1 vs 4+)? Linear or moving implementation? Length: time kills all deals. Don't overcomplicate small deals.
- **Industry expert**: study top trends, study greatness (coffee with experts), know customers, test knowledge (have leaders quiz you). Spend 10 minutes daily on trends.
- **Improve skills with DMAIC (Six Sigma)**: Define the problem (how can I book more meetings?); Measure and quantify (which part of prospecting?); Analyze root cause from data (worst conversion is email reply rate); Improve through pilots (A/B test); Control: validate, then iterate.

## ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)
Questions: company size; startup/SMB/enterprise; B2B vs B2C; industries; industry-agnostic vs specialized; geography; buying cycle and decision process; business model; shared pain points; past success patterns.
Actions: talk to 3 customers yourself (pain for the business and for them personally; why they chose you; how they cost-justified; benefits). Write a 1-2 sentence ICP statement. List 10 companies that match EXACTLY; if 9 of 10 variables match, drop it.

## Personas (know the difference between selling to a business and to a persona)
Talk to 3 customers about a day in the life. Outline everyone in the evaluation: who gets value directly, indirectly, and who would fear this hurts their job (plan for detractors). 2-3 goals per persona. Draft a prospecting message per persona (pain, goals, how you bridge the gap). Test messaging with friendly customers.

## Customer journey
1. Awareness: unconfirmed problem; not ready to be sold, ready to be made aware.
2. Consideration: known problem; exploring how to solve it (can't do it internally).
3. Purchase: taking a leap of faith; vendor chosen.
4. Retention: progress made; create a fan.
5. Problem solved: trust established; partnership foundation in place.
Match the conversation to the stage.

## Value proposition
Why is Solution A better for THIS persona than Solution B? It's not just "better"; it's better for that persona.

## Territory plan / QBR (template contents)
Past learnings and future strategies (what went well/didn't/strategies) -> quota/target math (product lines x # deals x avg size, amount to go, attainment %) -> Tier 1 / big-bet accounts (priorities, where time is spent, upcoming strategic meetings with goal and briefing doc) -> stakeholder map per deal (stakeholder, influence level, skeptic/neutral/fan, alignment) -> existing customer base strategy (current vs target investment, delta, strategy, help wanted) -> Tier 2 top 10 (target revenue, contact, attack plan, next step, help needed) -> Tier 3 strategy (don't let them slip, don't let them distract) -> help wanted (leadership, product, marketing, CS) -> appendix.

## QBR deck outline
Celebration/gratitude slide -> my purpose -> last quarter goals vs actuals (personal and professional) -> look-backs (accomplished / struggled) -> territory overview -> last-90-day funnel metrics (e.g., trial-to-win, demo scheduled-to-completed, demo completed-to-trial, connect-to-demo, call-to-connect) -> next quarter goals -> **the one decision you will hold yourself to** -> help needed (specific) -> questions. Be ready to defend every number.

## Mindset and qualification extras (from public interviews with Justin Jay Johnson)
- **Tripod**: conscience, craft, career. If one leg is missing it falls over; skill alone isn't enough. Most "technique" problems (objections, closing) are mindset problems first.
- **Reframe discomfort**: cold calls, interrupting, challenging someone exist because you can help them. Connect the activity to the outcome for the buyer.
- **Know who you serve and who you don't**: write a "big 10" list of what your ideal client does AND a "big 10" list of what disqualifies someone. Use both in prospecting and in Deal Inspection.
- **Sell only what you believe in.** Test it: how does it help people, and what measurable change does it create?
- **Values**: execute, evolve, enjoy. Celebrate micro-wins (a good call, a timely reply) so self-worth isn't only closed deals.
